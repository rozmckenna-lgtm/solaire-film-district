Where are you now?

Whether you have a screenplay, want to strengthen a project or want to understand the industry, the Creator Centre helps identify the next credible opportunity.

Different projects need different things

You do not need to be a producer to use Solaire PitchUp. A writer seeking their first producer should not be expected to arrive with the same materials as a production company seeking finance or distribution.

PitchUp identifies where a project currently stands, what the creator is seeking and what is genuinely required for the next stage.

  1. Project Ready
  2. Producer Ready
  3. Package Ready
  4. Finance Ready
  5. Market Ready

Projects can enter the PitchUp process at different points. The objective is to help each project become ready for its next credible opportunity, rather than burden creators with materials they do not yet need.

What happens if your project is accepted?

After the eligibility review, Solaire PitchUp confirms the most appropriate presentation and industry route.

  1. Confirm the next route. Identify what the project seeks and what its next stage genuinely requires.
  2. Provide relevant materials. The preparation guide identifies the documents and creative assets required for your project stage.
  3. Prepare your PitchUp package. PitchUp structures, edits and positions your materials for the appropriate catalogue and industry opportunities.

Exploring funding and finance

Development grants, film funds, regional and national support, tax credits, equity, broadcasters, pre-sales, international co-production, sponsorship, foundations and other routes may be appropriate depending on your project.
